Practical ways to burn more, sustainably:
• Walk 8,000–10,000 steps/day — set a step reminder in the Reminders tab.
• Swap deep-fried items (vada, poori, bonda) for steamed/grilled equivalents (idli, appam) a few times a week.
• Keep a moderate deficit (300–500 kcal/day) rather than a large one — easier to sustain and preserves muscle.
• Add 20–30 min of brisk walking or a home workout — log it under Steps & Activity.
• Prioritise protein (curd, sprouts, egg, fish) to stay full for longer.
